## About

**Bridging the gap between theoretical data science and bare-metal edge execution.**

MAH Quantum is a Bengaluru-based deep-tech group engineering specialized compute infrastructure for advanced AI and defense-grade systems. We work across foundational AI research, custom semiconductor and FPGA design, edge-AI hardware, and decision-support systems for public-sector and industrial clients.

Founded in 2023, operational since 2025, with a team of 47 across our group of entities.

Our mission: give engineers and research scholars real, hands-on access to production-grade AI tooling and hardware — not just theory. This GitHub organization is our public engineering registry.

## Projects

**ScorpioX D2**

A long-term initiative to build a frontier-scale AI system in India, from the ground up, at our Bengaluru facility.

**Quantum[-0-]Brain D25@1007**

An AI system built around persistent contextual memory, serving as the reasoning core for downstream decision-support applications across the group.

**ASURA Defence Intelligence System**

An AI-driven decision-support and command system for defense and public-sector applications.

**ASURA Defence System**

Field-deployable hardware units for defense and industrial equipment, secured with two-factor authentication, built to operate alongside command-level systems.

**Quantum D2 Drones**

A drone platform in two variants, supporting specialized logistics and transport use cases.

## Access & Open Source Philosophy

Active internal builds and scholar work-in-progress are kept in private repositories while under development. Once work clears internal review — and, for scholar projects, review aligned with our university partners' NAAC/NIRF quality frameworks — it's published here: deployment logs, quantization pipelines, and research whitepapers.

We're building this catalog out actively — check back as repositories go public, or follow our [research portal](https://research.mahquantum.tech) and [Zenodo community](https://zenodo.org/communities/mah-quantum) for published work.
